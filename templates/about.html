OUR MISSION

The HireLens Venture Story

Empowering students and job seekers with affordable, ethical, and explainable career tools.

🚀

Executive Summary

HireLens is a career-tech platform designed to eliminate the resume black hole experienced by millions of college students and fresh graduates. By comparing candidate resumes directly against targeted job postings using natural language heuristics and ethical AI, HireLens turns generic resumes into role-aligned applications in seconds.

🎯

Unique Selling Proposition

Unlike black-box resume builders or generic chatbots that hallucinate credentials, HireLens delivers an explainable alignment score, deterministic skill gap analysis, and honest tailoring with a strict Zero-Fabrication Policy.

Target Customer Segments

Multi-phase market expansion strategy

College Students & Interns

Undergraduates applying for competitive summer internships who need to tailor coursework and projects to technical job descriptions.

Fresh Graduates

Recent degree holders transitioning into full-time junior roles, requiring role-aligned terminology and quantifiable achievements.

Career Advisors & Placement Cells

Academic institutions and career development offices looking to boost overall placement readiness through transparent resume diagnostics.

🛡️ Our Ethical AI Commitment

Setting the standard for truthful, transparent career technology

1. Zero Fabrication

HireLens strictly forbids the invention of fake work experience, unearned degrees, fake companies, or fraudulent metrics.

2. Transparent Skill Gaps

When required skills are absent from a candidate's resume, our system explicitly advises adding them ONLY if genuinely possessed.

3. Explainable Scoring

Our alignment score is openly broken down into 4 clear weighted factors, avoiding deceptive promises of guaranteed interviews or employment.

Privacy Policy

How HireLens handles your uploaded resumes and data

In-Memory Processing: When you upload a resume, file contents are processed strictly in temporary server memory for the duration of the analysis request to calculate your alignment score and identify skills. We do not permanently store or archive your raw PDF or DOCX files on our servers.

Account Information: For registered users, we store your name, email, securely hashed password (via PBKDF2), and high-level analysis metadata (job title, alignment score, and skill counts) so that you can view your personal history.

No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or distribute candidate resumes or personal details to third-party recruiters or data brokers.

Terms of Service

Fair and honest use guidelines

By using HireLens, you agree to submit only your own resumes or authorized materials. You agree not to use HireLens to generate fraudulent or deceitful job applications.

HireLens provides educational diagnostic estimates and does not guarantee job placement, ATS passing scores, or employment offers.
