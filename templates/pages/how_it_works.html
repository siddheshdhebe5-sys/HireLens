THE PROCESS

How HireLens Works

A transparent, deterministic workflow designed to help you align your real experience with real job opportunities.

Step 01

Upload your resume

Upload your current master resume in PDF or DOCX format, or paste your text directly into the input field. HireLens extracts your contact information, education, experience, and projects using high-performance document parsing.

📄 In-memory parsing · No permanent document storage
Step 02

Add the job description

Paste the full job posting for the internship or position you are targeting. HireLens analyzes the requirements, qualifications, and responsibilities to detect core skills and domain terminology.

🎯 NLP terminology extraction · Target role metadata
Step 03

Improve with insights

Receive an explainable alignment score, see matched skills, uncover missing competencies, and review prioritized recommendations to strengthen your application.

📊 4-factor scoring · Zero fabrication policy

Our Explainable Scoring Methodology

No black boxes. Here is exactly how your alignment score is computed:

1. Required Skill Match (40%) 40% weight

Checks for the presence of exact technical and professional skills required by the job posting.

2. Keyword Alignment (25%) 25% weight

Evaluates domain-specific vocabulary and phrase frequency across both documents.

3. Semantic TF-IDF Fit (20%) 20% weight

Measures contextual similarity using mathematical cosine distance between document vectors.

4. Experience & Action Evidence (15%) 15% weight

Assesses the strength of action verbs, project scope, and quantified outcome statements.
