THE PROCESS

How HireLens Works

A straightforward, transparent 3-step workflow to prepare your resume for specific job openings.

Step 01

Upload your resume

Upload an existing resume in a supported format (PDF or DOCX), or paste your resume text directly into the secure editor.

Step 02

Add the job description

Paste the description of the internship or job you want, including requirements, qualifications, and core duties.

Step 03

Improve with insights

Review the alignment score, matching skills, gaps, and personalized suggestions to strengthen your real qualifications.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Clear Career Insights Without the Black Box

HireLens combines natural language skill extraction with explainable scoring heuristics to help you understand exactly how your resume connects with job requirements.

📊

Resume-Job Alignment Scoring

OUR COMMITMENT

Improve your resume without inventing your story.

HireLens is built to help you communicate your actual skills and experience more effectively. We believe genuine preparation is the only foundation for sustainable career growth.

Zero Qualification Fabrication

Recommendations never encourage users to claim qualifications, projects, or experience they do not have. Missing skills are presented as honest advisory signals.

In-Memory Document Processing

Uploaded resume files are parsed strictly in temporary server memory to calculate alignment metrics. We do not sell or distribute your private documents.

No False Guarantees

We provide explainable alignment estimates, not automated interview guarantees. Hiring decisions depend on hiring managers, interview performance, and verified capabilities.
